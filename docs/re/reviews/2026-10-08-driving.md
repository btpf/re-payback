# Driving review: original vs port (2026-10-08)

A line-by-line comparison of the vehicle physics against the port. The Mac OS X
build (`analysis/ghidra_macx/decompiled.c`, original C++ names) is the reference.
The Amiga builds have the same code: 68k Update 7 `FUN_000e3a58` vehicle branch,
`FUN_000a1758`, `FUN_000a241c`, `FUN_00044f82`; OS4 `FUN_0105f594` (re-decompiled in
`analysis/ghidra_os4/long_timeout.c`), `FUN_0105b9ec`, `FUN_01070868`, `FUN_010cd198`,
`FUN_010cbc7c`, `FUN_010cc288`, `FUN_010cc010`, `FUN_010ccc5c`. The 68k runs at 25 Hz,
the Mac, OS4 and the port at 30 Hz. The port runs one tick per frame (`ticks` = 1).

Mac field names: Object int index `[0..2]` x,y,z, `[3]` roll, `[4]` pitch, `[5]` heading,
`[9]` model, `[0x1f7..0x1f9]` vx,vy,vz, `+0x82c` health, `+0x890` speed, `+0x894` wheel
speed, `+0x808` yaw rate, `+0x80c` drive force, `+0x7e8` lean, `+0x7ec` dip, `+0x7f4..+0x800`
boat bob, `+0x804` last ground steer, `+0x818` on ground, `+0x81a` in water, `+0x8a3` J-turn
latch, `+0x8b0` level (max under the box), `+0x8b4` centre level, `+0x8b8..+0x8d8` 3×3
heights (`+0x8c8` = ground), `+0x8f0` last clear position, `+0x910` state, `+0x936` sunk
latch, `+0x938` moving, `+0x984` helicopter engine on. Model: `+0x8120` half-width (OS4
`+0x48c4`), `+0x8124` front, `+0x8128` rear, `+0x8134` max F, `+0x8138` max R, `+0x813c`
accel, `+0x8140` yaw, `+0x8144` grip, `+0x8148` mass, `+0x814c` brake, `+0x8160` RC car,
`+0x8184` tracked, `+0x8186` flies, `+0x8188` boat, `+0x818a` inverted lean, `+0x818c` bus.

Port line numbers are after the fixes (`crates/payback/src/sim/driving.rs` unless named).

## Order of one tick

`object_MainLoop` @ 1014a670 runs per object: `ResetObject` (stores the start x, y,
heading), `MoveObject_Vehicle` @ 1006e890, then `do_Other` @ 10146280, then
`RemoveCollideObj`/`AddCollideObj` @ 10055c70 (the scenery push). `MoveObject_Vehicle`
applies the helicopter lift and gravity, moves x/y by the velocity, runs
`CheckForCubeCollision` @ 100af980 and `BounceUnit` @ 10082bc0. `do_Other` then reads
the controls, updates the speed and, on the ground, steers, runs `PositionUnit` and
`SkidUnit`.

**Port before:** controls → steering → `position_unit` → grip → move/collide/bounce →
floor/landing (fresh `collide::floor`) → scenery. **Fix (done):** `drive` (driving.rs:322)
follows the original order: move (387) → controls (510) → airborne or ground step (612)
→ `skid_unit` → scenery (676).

## Findings

Each: original (Mac; Amiga) — port before — fix.

### Movement, gravity, ground (MoveObject_Vehicle / do_Other)

1. **Gravity on the ground.** Mac `MoveObject_Vehicle` @ 1006e890 (OS4 `FUN_0105b9ec`):
   `if (!inWater || !onGround) { vz += 0x20; z += vz }` on every tick, before the move.
   `do_Other` then snaps: `vz = clamp(ground + 0x80 − z, ±1000); z = ground`. So the
   collision runs at the z after gravity (on flat ground alternately ground and ground +
   0xa0), and a car leaving a ramp keeps `vz`. Port: no gravity on the ground; it tested
   walls at the snapped z. **Fixed** (driving.rs:389-396, 622-636).
2. **Ground = stored `+0x8c8`.** The ground, the level and the water flag come from the
   last clear collision step (`CheckForCubeCollision` writes them only without a hit). Port:
   a fresh `collide::floor` every tick, at the bounced position. **Fixed:** `floor_z =
   heights[4]`, levels and water from `box_heights` on clear steps only.
3. **Level `+0x8b0` is the highest level under the 9 box points** (`+0x8b4` is the centre
   level): `SkidUnit`'s surface, the scenery test and the effects use `+0x8b0`; water,
   crates and pickups `+0x8b4`. Port: the centre level for everything. **Fixed**
   (`box_heights` 1135, `Drive::centre_level`).
4. **Last clear position across pushes.** `+0x8f0` is never written by `CheckForCollisions`;
   the port dropped it whenever the car had been moved (contact pushes included). **Fixed:**
   contact.rs shifts the stored end position with each push, so only a teleport resets it.
5. **Clear position z** is the z at the collision test. Port stored it after the ground snap.
   **Fixed** (`clear_pos`).
6. **Landing** (do_Other, first sub-step): `vz < 0xa1 → vz = 0x80`, else HitScenery (0x1e)
   at volume `|vz|·20000`, pitch 15000, z + 100, `vz = 0x80 − |vz >> 2|`; `onGround = 1`,
   `z = ground`, sunk latch cleared. Port matched except the sound z. **Fixed.**
7. **Wall hit stops the car.** `BounceUnit` keeps the speed, but the tail of `do_Other` (Mac,
   after `SkidUnit`; OS4 main.c ~2503 `[0xec] = 0`) sets `speed = 0` on any hit flag and
   restores the pre-move heights and level. Port: halved the speed per axis, before the
   grip step. **Fixed** (driving.rs:668-673): speed 0 after the grip step, so this tick's
   velocity still converges on the old heading and the bounce velocity then decays at the
   grip rate. Heights already stay from the last clear step.
8. **BounceUnit details** (@ 10082bc0, OS4 `FUN_010cd198`): damage `max(v², 0) >> 14`, Thud 5
   (vol speed·10000, pitch 12000) or Thud2 0x4b (pitch 8000, dented `+0x93a`), bus driven
   by a player (state 2): Screams 0x4c, vol 0xff, pitch `15000 + random(2000)`. Port: the
   screams had no random pitch (an rng draw missing) and keyed on the camera target.
   **Fixed.** The rest matched. The port keeps `|speed|` for the volume (the original
   passes a signed speed; UNCERTAIN what `AddSound` does with a negative volume).
9. **Helicopter lift.** `if engineOn(+0x984) && z > 0 && vz > −0x100: vz −= 0xaa` before
   gravity, so a helicopter with its engine on climbs to z ≈ 0 and hovers. Port: missing
   (the flying helicopter drove on the ground). **Fixed** (389-391).

### Controls and throttle (do_Other @ 10146280; 68k ~124840-125170; OS4 main.c 1380-1640, 1784)

10. **Brake key.** Player (state 2) and RC car: the brake key sets the throttle to +1 when
    speed > 0, −1 when < 0, unchanged at 0. Port: the brake key only switched the grip to 4
    (a port invention) and never braked. **Fixed** (`player_vehicle_stick` 133).
11. **Grip 4 comes from the J-turn latch `+0x8a3`** (`SkidUnit` @ 10081830: `if latch == 1:
    grip = 4`; OS4 `FUN_010cc288`), not from the brake key. **Fixed** (`skid_unit`).
12. **Airborne throttle.** In the air (`z < +0x8c8`, not a flier) the throttle only revs the
    wheels: forward `force = ws < 1 ? brake : accel; ws += min(4·force, 2·maxF − ws)`,
    reverse the mirror with `2·maxR`; the speed is untouched (it still decays by 1 per tick
    without throttle). Port: accelerated the car in the air. **Fixed** (530-536, 557-560);
    the engine pitch uses the wheel speed.
13. **Brake / J-turn branches** match (force ramp 5, `+0x938` stop-at-zero, J-turn yaw
    `±2·yaw`, latch cleared each tick, not for tracked). Missing: `UseHandbrake` fires only
    for the first player's object (`cb24 == c60c[0]`); port fired for any camera target.
    **Fixed.** Missing: **tyre smoke while braking** — at each wheel (0.7 of the box
    extents: `cc0c/cc08 = cos/sin · 70/100`), `random(2) == 0`: type 1 `Dust` (Mac `c5ec`),
    pos `+ random(300)` per axis, vel `v/2 + random(50)`, size `min(|speed|·front >> 12,
    1000)` (disassembly 101482b8-101482e0), level `+0x8b0`, table 0. **Added**
    (`brake_smoke` 876).
14. **Damage speed cap** `clamp(H·max/50, max/4, max)` both ways: matches.
15. **Reversing beeper** (`+0x817c`, vol 10000000, pitch 0x5622): matches.
16. **Rocket car thrust frames.** With the throttle forward the model pointer swaps
    rocketcar_thrust1 ↔ thrust2 (from rocketcar or thrust2 → thrust1, thrust1 → thrust2),
    back to rocketcar otherwise (types 24-26 at `c57c+0x60/+100/+0x68`). The box extents
    follow the model. Port: not done. **Added** (`Drive::frames`, vehicles.rs spawn loads
    all three).
17. **Input gating.** In water only boats and fliers get input (line `if !inWater || boat ||
    flies`); a boat out of water gets none; the helicopter without the engine on gets none.
    Matches (port also drops input for parked/dead/burning, which the original does through
    the state switch). State 6 objects are skipped entirely by `object_MainLoop`; the port
    now does too.

### On the ground: dip, lean, steering, snap (do_Other; 68k ~125460-125750; OS4 main.c 2200-2490)

18. **Dip `+0x7ec`.** Relaxes 16·t toward 0; then throttle forward with speed < maxF: −16·t (so it sits at −16·t), floor
    −0x80; throttle back with speed > maxR: +16·t, cap 0x80. Added to the pitch target in
    `PositionUnit`. Port: the field existed but was never updated. **Fixed**
    (`ground_controls` 775).
19. **Lean `+0x7e8`.** Relaxes 32·t toward 0 every ground tick, then (no J-turn) slews 64·t
    toward `−steer·|speed|·256/maxF`, the other sign for `+0x818a` (boat). It is **added to
    the roll target** in `PositionUnit` (10081450: `arctan(..) + +0x7fc + +0x7e8`), up to
    256 units (16°). Port: lean of the other sign, relaxing 64, drawn as a separate 0.08 rad
    render tilt. **Fixed:** lean in the roll; `sync_vehicles` draws roll and pitch only.
20. **Steering only on the ground.** The yaw ramp, `heading += yaw`, and the 45° snap run
    only in the ground branch. Port: also in the air. **Fixed.** The formulas (yaw ramp 5,
    limit `tracked ? yaw : min(|speed|, yaw)`, zero without steer, snap `((h+0x168)/0x2d0)·
    0x2d0` by 4·t while speed ≠ 0; the snap's wrap branches are dead code) matched.
21. **Last steer `+0x804`** is stored each ground tick and used in the air (item 23).
    **Added.**

### Slopes (PositionUnit @ 10081450 = OS4 `FUN_010cbc7c`)

22. **Roll scale and arctan tables — CONFIRMED.** The roll ratio is multiplied by `0.00390625`
    (TOC `1017ceec` → 10174098, +0x38) = 1/256. `InitArctan` @ 10083050 and `arctan` @
    10083170 use the double table at TOC `1017cef8` → 101740e0 = {2880, 100, 10, 2^52+2^31,
    0, 1000, −10, −100, −1000, 255} and π (an import): `small[i] = (int)(2880·atan(i/100)/π)`,
    `big[i] = (int)(2880·atan(10 + i)/π)`, i < 1000; `arctan(x) = small[(int)(100x)]` for
    0 ≤ x < 10, `big[(int)(x − 10)]` to 1000, else 0x5a0, mirrored for x < 0. Port: 1/1000
    steps below 1 and whole units above (INFERRED). **Fixed** (collide.rs `arctan`). The
    `+0x7fc`/`+0x800` terms are the **boat bob** (item 27), 0 for every other vehicle.
    The rest of `PositionUnit` (row minima, crest rule, pitch from the centre line,
    `speed += pitch·ticks >> 8`, halfway easing) matched.

### In the air (PositionAirborneUnit @ 10082620 = OS4 `FUN_010ccc5c`, 68k `FUN_000a241c`)

23. Common: skid trails reset to −1, `onGround = 0`, sunk latch 0. Non-flier: **turns with the
    stored ground steer `+0x804`** (OS4 `[0xc9]`), by `min(|speed >> 1|, yaw·t)`, left raising
    the heading going forward; the nose tips 16·t (matches the port). Port: steered with the
    live stick through the ground code. **Fixed** (`position_airborne_unit` 831).
24. **Flying helicopter:** `ws = speed`, level `+0x8b0 = 2`, velocity pulled toward the
    heading by the model grip·t (no surface), yaw ramp like a tracked vehicle on the live
    steer, `pitch = speed`, `roll = 0`. Port: only pitch/roll. **Fixed.**
25. **Engine pitch** in the air: `|ws|·20000 / maxF + 6000` (tank `·10000 + 4000`), the plain
    top speed; on the ground the damage-capped one, with the clonk swap (sample 1 → 0x2e,
    0x43-0x46 → 0x47-0x4a below 50 health, ground only). Port: speed instead of wheel speed,
    capped top speed always. **Fixed** (`vehicle_sounds` 1217).

### Grip, skids (SkidUnit @ 10081830 = OS4 `FUN_010cc288` + `FUN_010cc010`, 68k `FUN_000a1758`)

26. Called **only on the ground** (and after the sinking branch). Port: always (the car
    gripped in the air). **Fixed.** Details:
    - The target `speed·(sin, cos) >> 8` is taken **before** the surface bleeds the speed.
      Port: after. **Fixed.**
    - Surface = flag of the top texture on level `+0x8b0` of the centre square, 4 off the map
      or level > 2; no visibility test. Port: tested face visibility. **Changed** to match.
    - Grip table and bleeds matched (0x22 g/2 & 450/3, 0x24 2g & 350/20, 0x32 4 & 200/10,
      0x33 g·10/25, 0x34 1, default g·4/5).
    - Slip `2·(|Δvx| + |Δvy|)/t` after the pull, clamp 0..255, ×2 in water; skipped entirely
      for fliers. Port: zeroed slip for tracked vehicles; the original still smokes for the
      tank and only mutes the sound. **Fixed** (`skid_audible`).
    - **Skid marks, smoke, spray were missing.** Added per the original: bus screams when a
      skid starts (player state 2, wheel 0 trail −1); not for boats: per wheel (−hw, +hw) ×
      (−rear, front): on land and not tracked, type-8 marks from the last trail point (or the
      wheel's position at the tick start), `n = max(|dx|,|dy|)/(size·7/8) + 1` points, size
      0xe6 (RC 100), level `+0x8b0`: grass/sand `DirtSkidmark` table 0x30000, snow/slush/ice
      `Dust` table 0, else `Dust`(shadow) table 0x10000; then `random(2) < t`: a type-1 puff
      at `+ random(300)`, vel `v/2 + random(50)`: grass/sand `Dirt` 0x30000 size `slip·hw>>7`,
      snow `Dust` `>>7`, else `Dust` `>>8`; in water instead `random(2) == 0`: spray `Dust`,
      size `500 + random(500)`, vel `v/4 + random(50)`, level + 1. Skid sound (2, handle `+0x91c`):
      vol `slip·40000`, pitch `slip·10 + 10000`, z + 250, or 0 when tracked or in water;
      not updated in the air (keeps playing). (`skid_unit` 995.)

### Water (do_Other)

27. **Boat bob.** `CreateObject` @ 10075630 sets `+0x7f4 = −25`, `+0x7f8 = −11` for boats; on
    water each tick `+0x7fc += +0x7f4`, `+0x800 += +0x7f8`, each rate ±2 toward 0 (a spring),
    added to the roll and pitch targets. With the throttle, a wake of 2·t type-8 `Dust`
    puffs behind the stern (`start + k·(pos − start)`, k = 1..2t, `− rear·(sin,cos)`, ±50,
    z −100..100, vel `v ∓ (sin,cos)/2 + random(300) − 150`, vz `random(10) − 5`, size
    `0x82 + random(50)`, table 0). Port: missing. **Added** (`boat_bob` 906).
28. **Splash** (on the ground in water, latch `+0x936`): RC car `health = −71`,
    `ObjectSunk`, Splash 0x2b vol mass·10000 (8000 in later sub-steps), pitch 0x209e. The
    water flag is the centre cube's top type 4, set on clear steps **whether or not the car
    is down** (the port required z ≥ floor; changed to the original).
29. **Sinking.** Non-boat in water on the ground: speed 0, `pitch += 4·t` while the raw
    pitch < 0x5a0 (port compared the signed angle; fixed), and while z < 27000: z += 32·t
    with bubbles: for each of the 9 rotated box points `random(5) == 1`: type 5 `Bubble`,
    ±250, vel `(random(10) − 5, random(10) − 5, random(100) − 300)`, size `100 +
    random(150)`, level + 1. At 27000: a player's car loses the player ("Drowned!" 2018,
    `ShowRecord`, respawn timer 80, a multiplayer life), `ObjectDead`, state 6. Port: sank
    forever, no bubbles, no drowning. **Added** (`sinking` 942; the `Bubble` tile in
    effects.rs).

31. **Helicopter half-width / 3** (Mac 22614: `+0x8120 /= 3`, door `+0x8150 = −hw`): port used

32. **Props:** the dummy's half-width is halved, the bullet's mass is 10 (port 1). **Fixed.**

### Wall collision (CheckForCubeCollision @ 100af980 vehicle branch)

Verified to match line for line (sub-agent review): box points, probe depths (dz offset only
when raising), map-edge clamp hits, edge tests 0↔6, 2↔8, 0↔2, 6↔8 then the centre path,
`MergeCollide`, `GetCollideInfo`, `GetCubeInfo`/`GetCubeInfo2`, the clear-step crates and
pickups, the N7 heading deflection. Differences were all at the call site (items 1-5).
Left: glass is removed at the end of the tick, not the moment it breaks (rare; a second
face of the same cube can still block within the tick).

### Object contacts (CheckForCollisions @ 1006a150; OS4 `FUN_01056c58`, 68k `FUN_000de0b6`)

Matching: pair order, 4 circles at `{0, hw−rear, front−hw, 2·front/3−hw}`, `R = (hwA+hwB)·
17>>4`, z halved and gated at 20000, push split by the smaller mass, damage formula and
shifts, run-over thresholds, Ow, Irony bonus, Thud volumes. Fixed in `contact.rs`:

34. **Speed damping**: per touching pair `s = (8s + s·m_self/(mi+mj))/9` for both, before the
    damage (and the run-over test reads the damped speed). Was missing.
35. **z push** on the velocity too (`±pz·min(m)/m`). Was damage-only.
36. **Integer math**: |dx|,|dy| < 25001 gate, `dist = (int)sqrt(d²)` at least 1 (a zero
    distance counts), `px += dx·R/dist − dx`, `pz += (dz·R/dist − dz)·2`, `dz = Δz >> 1`.
    Port used f64 and skipped d = 0.
37. **Live positions**: circles from each object's current position per pair (pushes of
    earlier pairs included). Port computed all circles once.
38. **Radius**: raw `+0x8120` (port `max(64)`), helicopter / 3 (item 31).
39. **RC car** takes no part in contacts (`+0x8160`). Port included it.
40. **Sparks**: only two vehicles, either faster than 50, one per touching circle pair:
    type 9 `Flare` between the circles ±200, z `zi + dz/2 ± 200`, vel `random(80) − 40 −
    mass-weighted velocity` (vz `− (vzi+vzj)/2`), size `100 + random(50)`, j's level + 1.
    Port: one per damaged victim at the victim, zero velocity.
41. **Vehicle-vehicle sounds**: for both vehicles every touching tick by each one's damage
    (Thud 5 / Thud2 0x4b, volume `(|px|+|py|)·100000`, pitch 15000/10000), Thud2 dents; a bus
    driven by a player screams (random pitch). Port: only damaged victims, any class.
42. **Run-over victims**: damage, stop and blood even at 0 damage once the speed test
    passes; blood = type-8 splat ±100 (size `16·min(dmg,50) + 50 + random(50)` for j,
    `100 + random(150)` for i, on the level) plus 3 type-9 droplets (size `8·min + 80 +
    random(2·min)`, vel `random(80) − 40 − v/4`, level + 1). Port: one splat, no scatter.

Not done (documented, outside the vehicle handling): the ram score (`+0x864 += mult·
min(6000, max|p·m/m|² >> 9)` and `CheckHitObject` when the attacker has an owner), the shunt
counter `+0x944` per vehicle contact tick, barrier/cone knock-over (sound 30, `[3] =
0x5a0`, extra `v ∓= p/4`), the dummy taking no push (the other side takes all), the touch
flag for non-player walkers, and `Hurt` at zero damage.

## Left uncertain

- `AddSound` with a negative volume (BounceUnit passes the signed speed). Port uses |speed|.
- π for `InitArctan` is an imported symbol (`1017c6f8` resolves at load); 2880/π is implied
  by the 0x5a0 saturation.
- Invisible top faces: the original reads their texture's surface flag anyway; the port now
  does the same (the map stores a texture for every face).
- The skid sound id and the effect tables were checked against `addeffect_calls.tsv` and the
  OS4 decompile; the Mac `Dust`/`Dust`(shadow) mapping is from `InitObjects_Effects` @
  100730b0 and `InitAll` (`c5ec`/`c66c` = Dust, `c4ac`/`c668` = Dust with the shadow flag,
  `c660` DirtSkidmark, `c65c` Dirt, `c3bc` Bubble).
